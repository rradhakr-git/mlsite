Applied Machine Learning Systems & Architectures

Introduction

This site presents a set of applied machine learning proof-of-concepts focused on how models are **built, composed, and used in real systems**.

The projects move from domain-specific intent classification, through multimodal document understanding and agent-based orchestration, to decision support under uncertainty.

Each POC is designed end-to-end, with attention to data assumptions, model behavior, system boundaries, and operational trade-offs. The emphasis is not on isolated techniques, but on how **ML components fit together** to produce reliable, actionable outcomes.
